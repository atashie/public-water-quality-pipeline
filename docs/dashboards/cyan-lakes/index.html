Weekly index, 7-day maximum composites

Median, 90th percentile, and maximum of the index codes of the pixels inside the lake. The dashed line is the bloom line from the Map tab. The faint dotted lines mark where the named levels of estimated abundance start. A week with no value leaves a gap. Click a week to add it to the comparison below.
Each bar is one week, split into the share of the lake's pixels with a value, without a value, and marked as land by the CyAN land mask. Coverage is the blue share. Land-marked pixels never have a value, so some lakes never reach 100 percent.

Each year's weekly median, by time of year

One line per year, each week plotted at the date it starts. Older years are lighter. The newest year is in teal. 2016 to 2018 are dotted: Sentinel-3B joined during 2018, and those years have a smaller share of weeks with a value, see why. Weeks without a value leave gaps. Click a year in the legend to hide it, or double-click to show that year alone. Click a week to add it to the comparison below.
